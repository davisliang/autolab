## AutoMix (Aggarwal et al., 2023)

AutoMix routes queries between LLMs by combining few-shot self-verification with a POMDP-based router. The POMDP formulates routing as a sequential decision problem where self-verification confidence scores are noisy observations of latent query difficulty. The router selects the appropriately-sized model at each step and terminates when sufficient confidence is achieved.

**Relevance to HYP-004**: AutoMix is the closest work to framing LLM routing as a formal decision process (POMDP = partially observable MDP). It is RL-adjacent but not RL-trained: the POMDP policy is solved via value iteration over a fixed model, not learned end-to-end via policy gradient from reward signals. HYP-004's RL router learns the routing policy from scalar quality-cost rewards via REINFORCE, which does not require white-box access to self-verification and can generalize the quality-cost tradeoff through λ sweeping. AutoMix is only evaluated on 2-model setups; HYP-004 extends to 3 tiers.

**Key claims**:
1. POMDP router reduces cost by >50% at matched performance across 5 datasets and 5 models.
2. Self-verification as an entailment problem improves calibration over raw confidence scores.
3. POMDP can handle unsolvable queries (routes no model) — a cost saving not in FrugalGPT/RouteLLM.
4. POMDP policy is solved analytically, not trained via policy gradient.
5. Requires few-shot examples (≥50) per task; not zero-shot generalizable out of the box.
