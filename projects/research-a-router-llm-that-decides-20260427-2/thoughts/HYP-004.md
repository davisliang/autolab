## Hypothesis HYP-004: RL-Tuned Routing Policy with Quality-Cost Reward

### Claim
Supervised routing trains a classifier to predict a fixed "correct" tier per query. This bakes in a specific quality-cost tradeoff decided at label-time. An RL routing policy, trained with scalar reward `r = quality_score - λ · cost_ratio` (where λ controls the cost penalty), learns a *flexible* Pareto-optimal policy. At the operating point where quality ≥ 0.80 normalized vs always-large, the RL policy achieves ≥10% higher cost-normalized quality (CNQ) than the best supervised baseline.

### Motivation
Supervised routing requires choosing a quality-cost operating point at training time via labeling strategy. RL directly optimizes the tradeoff objective, allowing the policy to find shortcuts a human labeler wouldn't anticipate — e.g., routing an entire class of queries that look complex to the medium tier when medium quality is within tolerance.

### RL Setup
- **Policy**: Lightweight router MLP (same encoder as HYP-003) with a 3-way softmax output (small/medium/large).
- **Reward**: `r = Q(query, model_tier) - λ · C(tier)` where Q is normalized task score from pre-cached responses, C is normalized inference cost (FLOPs proxy: 1x / 4x / 16x for small/medium/large), λ swept in {0.1, 0.3, 0.5, 1.0}.
- **Algorithm**: REINFORCE with mean-reward baseline, 50 epochs, lr=1e-4.
- **Baseline**: Supervised GBDT router (HYP-001) trained with labels from the tier that first achieves ≥95% of large-model quality.
- **Evaluation**: Sweep operating points by varying λ; compare CNQ at quality floor 0.80 and area-under-Pareto-curve.

### Pre-registered Prediction
- **metric**: `cnq_improvement_over_supervised_at_quality_floor_0.80` — CNQ_rl - CNQ_supervised, both evaluated at the operating point where quality retention ≥ 0.80.
- **threshold**: 0.10 (≥10 percentage-point improvement in CNQ)
- **direction**: greater

### Why This Is Novel
FrugalGPT uses cascades but no RL policy learning. LLM-Blender uses supervised fusion. RouteLLM uses binary classification. None formulate routing as an RL problem with a continuous quality-cost reward, allowing adaptation to arbitrary cost budgets at inference time via the λ knob.
