## LitFinding LIT-014: RouteLLM (Ong et al., 2024)

**State-of-the-art baseline for HYP-003**: RouteLLM represents the current best-practice learned routing approach, trained directly on human preference data. It achieves 2× cost savings on standard benchmarks. However, all four router architectures (matrix factorization, BERT classifier, SW ranker, causal LLM) are single deterministic models with no uncertainty quantification.

**Absence of ensemble uncertainty**: RouteLLM uses a threshold on its single router's score to make a hard binary decision. Near the threshold, this is precisely where calibration matters most — a miscalibrated score near the boundary causes the most routing errors. HYP-003 targets exactly this regime.

**Key metric gap**: RouteLLM reports aggregate accuracy-cost trade-off (APGR, CPT) but does not separately analyze *hard-query* routing error — the regime where HYP-003 claims a ≥20% error reduction. The hard-query subset analysis is the novel evaluation axis.
