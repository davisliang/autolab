## Hypothesis HYP-003: Uncertainty-Aware Router via Ensemble Disagreement (Cross-Domain from Active Learning)

### Cross-Domain Origin
In **active learning**, ensemble disagreement — measuring how much a committee of models disagrees on a label — is the gold-standard for identifying the most informative (hardest) unlabeled examples (Query by Committee, Seung et al. 1992; deep ensemble calibration, Lakshminarayanan et al. 2017). We transplant this to routing: high disagreement among a committee of lightweight routing classifiers signals that the query sits near a decision boundary, and such queries should be escalated.

### Claim
A committee of N=5 lightweight routing classifiers (tiny MLP heads on top of a shared frozen encoder, trained with different random seeds) produces a disagreement score per query. Queries above the 80th percentile disagreement threshold are escalated to the large model; the rest follow the majority-vote routing decision. This reduces routing error on "hard" queries (those where the correct tier is medium or large but a single classifier would mis-route) by ≥20% relative to a single deterministic classifier baseline.

### Why This Matters
Single-point routing classifiers are overconfident near decision boundaries — exactly where routing errors are most costly (sending a hard question to the smallest model). Calibrated uncertainty lets the system be conservative on ambiguous queries without escalating everything.

### Experimental Setup
- **Dataset**: Same stratified sample. Hard queries defined as those where small model quality < 0.7 × large model quality.
- **Router committee**: 5 lightweight 2-layer MLPs (128 hidden units) on top of frozen all-MiniLM-L6-v2 encoder, trained with different seeds.
- **Disagreement score**: Entropy of the distribution over routing decisions across committee members.
- **Baseline**: Single MLP classifier (same architecture as one committee member) and GBDT from HYP-001.
- **Metric**: Hard-query routing error = fraction of hard queries incorrectly routed to a tier below the needed tier.

### Pre-registered Prediction
- **metric**: `hard_query_routing_error_reduction` — (error_rate_single_classifier - error_rate_uncertainty_router) / error_rate_single_classifier on the hard-query subset.
- **threshold**: 0.20 (≥20% relative reduction in hard-query routing error)
- **direction**: greater

### Key Novelty
No prior LLM routing paper uses ensemble disagreement as a routing signal. Prior work uses single-model confidence, which is known to be poorly calibrated. This hypothesis directly tests calibration-aware routing.
