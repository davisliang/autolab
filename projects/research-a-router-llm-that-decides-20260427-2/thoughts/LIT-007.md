## LitFinding LIT-012: Deep Bayesian Active Learning (Gal et al., 2017)

**Cross-domain origin for HYP-003**: HYP-003 explicitly transplants the active learning insight that *epistemic uncertainty* identifies the most informative (hardest) examples. This paper is the canonical demonstration: BALD acquisition using a committee of stochastic forward passes (MC dropout) cuts the labeling budget by more than half compared to random or deterministic selection.

**Why this matters for routing**: The "hard queries" in LLM routing (where a small model would fail but a large model would succeed) are analogous to the "most informative examples" in active learning — both lie near decision boundaries. Gal et al. show definitively that Bayesian (multi-sample) uncertainty detects these boundary cases while a deterministic model's softmax confidence fails to.

**Key quantitative result**: BALD achieves 5% MNIST error with 295 labeled images vs 835 for random sampling — a 2.8× reduction. The analogous improvement in routing would be a large reduction in hard-query error when using committee disagreement vs single-model confidence.

**Gap for HYP-003**: The setting is label acquisition, not model selection for inference. The routing application (use uncertainty to decide *which model to call*, not *which example to label*) is the novel contribution.
