## Relevance to HYP-002

Directly validates the confidence signal design in HYP-002. HYP-002 uses `exp(mean(log-probs of output tokens))` as the cascade escalation signal. This paper shows that (a) token-level probabilities from large models are well-calibrated, and (b) models' self-assessed confidence correlates with actual correctness. However, calibration degrades out-of-distribution and after RLHF fine-tuning—a risk for commercial APIs like Claude Haiku which are RLHF-tuned. Thresholds τ₁, τ₂ must be validated on a diverse held-out set.
