## Relevance to HYP-002

RouteLLM is the primary **upfront router baseline** HYP-002 claims to beat. RouteLLM explicitly notes that cascade approaches (FrugalGPT, AutoMix) suffer from additive latency on escalated queries—one of HYP-002's stated risks. RouteLLM's 2x cost reduction on MMLU/MT-Bench sets the empirical bar that HYP-002's cascade must exceed by ≥15% on cost-normalized quality. If RouteLLM's single-query approach already achieves near-optimal routing, the cascade's main advantage (self-triage on easy queries) may be marginal.
