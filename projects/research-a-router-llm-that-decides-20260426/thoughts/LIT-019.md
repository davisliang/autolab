## Summary

Hybrid LLM trains a DeBERTa encoder to predict the quality gap between small and large LLMs per query. Soft labels from 10 sampled responses improve over hard labels. A tunable threshold controls the quality-cost tradeoff at inference time.

## Relevance to HYP-001

Most architecturally similar to HYP-001: pre-generation binary classifier with negligible overhead, evaluated on quality-cost tradeoff. HYP-001 proposes replacing DeBERTa embeddings with explicit syntactic/semantic features (TTR, parse depth, task type) + GBT and extending to ternary routing.

## Gap

No ablation of sparse query features vs. DeBERTa embeddings. Binary routing only. HYP-001 directly fills both gaps.
