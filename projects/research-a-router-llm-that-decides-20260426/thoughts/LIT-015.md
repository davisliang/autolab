## Relevance to HYP-002

Self-consistency provides an alternative cascade confidence signal: run Haiku k times and escalate only when answers disagree. More expensive than single log-prob (k Haiku calls) but likely better calibrated. Sets up HYP-002's log-prob approach as a cheaper approximation to this sampling-based oracle. If log-prob confidence underperforms at routing, multi-sample agreement is the obvious next design point.
