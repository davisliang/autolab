## Summary

RouteLLM (UC Berkeley / Anyscale, 2024) is the most comprehensive LLM router framework to date. It leverages human preference data from Chatbot Arena to train routers that generalize across LLM pairs. The framework is open-sourced and evaluated on MMLU, MT Bench, and GSM8K.

## Relevance to HYP-003

RouteLLM represents the **best current static router**. Its generalization experiments (routing between LLMs not seen in training) show robustness to model-level shift but do **not** address query-distribution shift (e.g., switching from code queries to medical questions mid-deployment).

The preference-data training paradigm also requires expensive annotation, whereas HYP-003 proposes online adaptation with only 10%-sampled LLM judge calls.

## Gap identified

No online adaptation to query-domain shift. Relies on expensive offline preference annotation. No evaluation of degradation after abrupt domain shift.
