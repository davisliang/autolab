## Relevance to HYP-004

HYP-004 computes token-level entropy over k=5 Haiku completions. Kuhn et al. demonstrate that this token-level entropy is confounded by paraphrase diversity—a model that generates semantically identical answers in different surface forms appears uncertain when it is actually confident. For routing on factual QA tasks (MMLU), this is a serious concern: Haiku may generate paraphrased correct answers, causing high entropy that incorrectly flags easy queries as hard.

## Practical implication

HYP-004 could adopt a lightweight semantic clustering step (using a small NLI model or embedding cosine-similarity threshold) before computing entropy. This would increase labeling cost slightly but improve label quality. The experiment design should test both raw-entropy labels and semantic-entropy labels as ablations.

## Limitation of concern

The semantic entropy method requires an NLI classifier at label-generation time—this adds a dependency beyond pure Haiku sampling. For the self-supervised labeling pipeline in HYP-004, this is tractable but should be documented.
