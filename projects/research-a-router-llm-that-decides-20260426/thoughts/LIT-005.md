## Summary

FrugalGPT (Stanford, 2023) establishes the LLM cascade paradigm: route queries through a sorted list of APIs (cheapest first), escalating only when a learned scoring function deems the current answer unreliable. It demonstrates remarkable cost savings on fixed-distribution tasks.

## Relevance to HYP-003

FrugalGPT is the primary **static baseline** against which HYP-003 competes. Its explicit limitation — "training examples should be from the same or similar distribution as the test examples" — is precisely the vulnerability that HYP-003's BOLA-inspired online adaptation addresses.

The absence of any domain shift experiment in FrugalGPT is a clear gap in the literature.

## Gap identified

No online re-calibration mechanism; the cascade strategy is trained once and deployed statically. Quality degrades under distribution shift.
