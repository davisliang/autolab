## Relevance to HYP-002

CALM is the within-model analogue of HYP-002's cross-model cascade. Both use token-level confidence to decide when to stop escalating compute. CALM shows that softmax-based confidence signals can be reliably calibrated for quality guarantees—directly supporting HYP-002's use of `exp(mean(log-probs))` as the escalation signal. Key difference: CALM exits at an intermediate layer of a single model; HYP-002 escalates to a different (larger) model entirely. CALM's LTT calibration framework could be adapted to set τ₁, τ₂ thresholds with statistical guarantees.
