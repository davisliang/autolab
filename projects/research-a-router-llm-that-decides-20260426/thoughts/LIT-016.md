## Relevance to HYP-002

Speculative decoding instantiates the 'cheap-model-first' pattern at the token level within a single response. HYP-002's cascade operates at the response level across separate API models. Key analogy: speculative decoding's acceptance rate (fraction of cheap tokens kept) ≈ HYP-002's non-escalation rate. When the cheap model is calibrated to the target, most tokens are accepted—suggesting Haiku's log-probs may be a good proxy for whether Sonnet/Opus would give a different answer.
