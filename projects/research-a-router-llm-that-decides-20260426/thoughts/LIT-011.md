## Relevance to HYP-002

FrugalGPT is the closest prior work. HYP-002's τ₁/τ₂ threshold mechanism mirrors FrugalGPT's cascade exactly, but uses **token log-prob** as the confidence signal (no separate trained scorer), and compares against a **multi-signal upfront router** (HYP-001) rather than a single-model baseline. The 15% cost-normalized quality improvement claim in HYP-002 is more modest than FrugalGPT's 98% cost claim but is measured on a harder multi-benchmark (MMLU + GSM8K + HumanEval) with a stronger baseline.

Key gap: FrugalGPT trains a separate scorer; HYP-002 tests whether the model's own token log-probs are sufficient — avoiding extra training cost and API overhead.
