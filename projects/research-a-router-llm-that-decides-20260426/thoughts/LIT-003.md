## Relevance to HYP-004

Self-consistency's core insight—that answer agreement across samples signals correctness and thus (implicitly) difficulty—is the conceptual ancestor of HYP-004's entropy labeling. HYP-004 operationalizes this: high answer-set entropy (low agreement) → hard → route to Opus; low entropy (high agreement) → easy → route to Haiku.

## Key difference

Self-consistency uses agreement to *answer* the query; HYP-004 uses agreement to *label difficulty* for router training. This is a novel combination: using the self-consistency signal not to directly answer, but to generate training labels for a lightweight downstream classifier.

## Practical concern

Self-consistency evidence suggests k≥5 samples are needed for reliable majority-vote estimates on hard benchmarks. HYP-004 proposes exactly k=5, which may be marginal for noisy tasks. The experiment should test sensitivity to k (3, 5, 10).
