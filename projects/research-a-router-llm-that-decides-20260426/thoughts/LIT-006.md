## Relevance to HYP-004

FrugalGPT's generation scoring function is the supervised counterpart of HYP-004's self-supervised entropy labeler. Where FrugalGPT requires human-labeled correctness to train g(q,a), HYP-004 proposes to replace this with unsupervised entropy labels from Haiku sampling. If HYP-004 succeeds, it provides a label-free alternative to FrugalGPT's scoring function—bootstrapping the cascade without any labeled data.

## Key comparison point

In the experiment, FrugalGPT (trained on human labels) should be one of the supervised baselines. The gap between FrugalGPT and the entropy-labeled router is the practical cost of self-supervision in routing accuracy.

## Important methodological note

FrugalGPT trains on per-domain labeled data; the entropy labeler in HYP-004 generates domain-agnostic labels. Whether the entropy labeler generalizes as well as FrugalGPT's supervised scorer across domains is the crux of HYP-004's novelty claim.
