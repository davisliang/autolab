## Summary

RouteLLM trains binary routers from human pairwise preference labels (Chatbot Arena). BERT-style encoders outperform other architectures. Data augmentation is key. The open-source framework is evaluated on MMLU and MT-Bench — the same benchmarks as HYP-001.

## Relevance to HYP-001

Most direct competitor: pre-generation binary router, evaluated on MMLU. HYP-001 targets ternary routing (Haiku/Sonnet/Opus) with lightweight GBT on interpretable features, trading representational capacity for speed (<5ms vs ~20ms) and interpretability.

## Gap

No ablation of which features matter; BERT black box. HYP-001's explicit features (TTR, parse depth, task type) would reveal what drives routing decisions. If GBT matches BERT at 85%+, lower latency and interpretability are significant advantages.
