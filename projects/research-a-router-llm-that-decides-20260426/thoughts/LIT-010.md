## Summary

LLM-Blender demonstrates the fundamental heterogeneity of LLM capabilities — no model is uniformly best across query types. This provides motivation for per-query adaptive routing (as in HYP-003) over always-use-one-model approaches.

## Relevance to HYP-003

The key finding that optimal LLM varies by query (and by domain) directly motivates HYP-003's claim: after a domain shift, a previously calibrated router will systematically route to the wrong tier. BOLA-style online re-calibration is needed to track the changing optimal tier.

LLM-Blender's approach of querying all models is orthogonal to routing — it provides an upper bound on achievable quality but is too expensive for production. HYP-003 targets single-model-per-query efficiency.

## Gap identified

Ensemble approaches are cost-prohibitive for production routing. No evaluation of domain shift impact on model selection accuracy. No online adaptation to changing query distributions.
