## Motivation

Supervised routing requires labeled examples of "which model was best for this query" — an expensive annotation process. Labels are also indirect: they encode which model *happened* to produce the best output on measured examples, not which model is *optimal in expectation* for queries of that type.

RL-based routing sidesteps both problems:
1. **No annotation bottleneck**: The reward is computed on-the-fly by an LLM judge (e.g., a small fine-tuned quality classifier or GPT-4o-mini) rating the response quality.
2. **Direct optimization**: The policy optimizes quality-cost tradeoff directly rather than fitting proxy labels.

## Mechanism

1. **Router policy**: 3-class softmax head on top of a frozen sentence-transformer (all-MiniLM-L6-v2). Parameters: ~1M.
2. **Training loop** (REINFORCE with baseline):
   - Sample query from dataset
   - Sample model choice from policy distribution
   - Call chosen model, get response
   - Score with reward = Q(response) - λ * cost(model_choice), where Q ∈ [0,1] is LLM-judge quality
   - Update policy with REINFORCE gradient: ∇θ log π(a|s) * (r - b), baseline b = running mean reward
3. **Cost budget sweep**: train 5 policies with λ ∈ {0.1, 0.5, 1.0, 2.0, 5.0} to trace the Pareto frontier.
4. **Judge**: fine-tuned DeBERTa-v3 quality classifier on a held-out response quality dataset (avoid GPT-4o API cost in training loop).

## Why RL beats supervised routing

- Supervised labels are noisy (best model on seen response ≠ best model in expectation).
- RL policy can discover non-obvious routing heuristics (e.g., short math queries → Sonnet, not Haiku, because Haiku drops precision).
- Directly optimizes the quality-cost tradeoff at each λ level.

## Experimental Setup

- Dataset: FLAN-v2 (1000 training queries), MMLU + GSM8K (evaluation)
- Training: 200 REINFORCE gradient steps per λ value, batch size 16
- Baseline: RouteLLM (best supervised variant from their paper) at matched budget
- Metric: pareto_dominance_count = number of budget levels (5) where RL policy Pareto-dominates supervised baseline (higher quality at same cost)
- Seeds: [42, 123, 456]
- Expected: RL dominates at ≥3 of 5 budget levels

## Open Questions

- Is REINFORCE variance manageable with a 1M-param policy? May need PPO or advantage actor-critic.
- Can the DeBERTa judge be replaced with rule-based quality scoring (e.g., exact match for MMLU) to avoid the judge training cost?
