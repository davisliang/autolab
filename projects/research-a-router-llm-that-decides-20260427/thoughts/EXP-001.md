## Experiment: Speculative Cascade vs Input-Feature Routing (HYP-002)

### Design

Both routing strategies are swept across operating points (judge strictness for proposed, routing threshold for baseline). The primary comparison is at **matched quality**: the best cost savings each method achieves while staying within 2pp of always-Sonnet accuracy on the same difficulty draw.

**Proposed (speculative cascade)**:
1. Every query runs Haiku (cost=1.0) + lightweight judge (cost=0.2)
2. Judge decides accept/reject based on TPR/FPR operating point
3. Rejected queries escalate to Sonnet (cost=5.0)
4. Sweep TPR: 0.60→0.95, FPR: 0.02→0.15 (correlated)

**Baseline (input-feature router)**:
1. Router estimates difficulty with Gaussian noise (σ=0.10)
2. Routes to Haiku or Sonnet based on threshold
3. Sweep threshold: 0.10→0.55
4. No judge overhead — routes before generation

### Simulation Model
- N=1319 questions (GSM8K test set size)
- Difficulty ~ Beta(2,3), mean≈0.40
- Haiku solves with P = sigmoid(-(d-0.35)/0.05), ~43% overall accuracy
- Sonnet solves with P = sigmoid(-(d-0.60)/0.05), ~80% overall accuracy
- Shared model outcomes across sweeps for fair comparison

### Key Structural Insight
Speculative cascade pays Haiku+judge (1.2 units) on ALL queries, even escalated ones. Escalated queries cost 6.2 total vs 5.0 for direct Sonnet. This structural overhead is the critical test: does the information advantage (seeing output) overcome the cost of always running the cheap model?

### Metrics
- **cost_savings_pp_vs_feature_router**: proposed savings minus baseline savings at matched quality (pp). Hypothesis threshold: ≥8pp.
- **accuracy_delta_vs_sonnet**: proposed accuracy minus Sonnet-only accuracy (pp). Must be ≥ -2pp.
- **proposed_savings_pp**: absolute cost savings of proposed vs always-Sonnet
- **baseline_savings_pp**: absolute cost savings of baseline vs always-Sonnet

### Seeds
[42, 123, 456] — 3 independent difficulty draws × model outcome draws.

### Sanity Gate
Simulates judge training on 32 examples; verifies cross-entropy loss drops ≥50%.
