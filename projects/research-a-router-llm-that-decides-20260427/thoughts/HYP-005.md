## Motivation

HYP-001 proposes using Haiku's token-level entropy as an escalation signal. But entropy is a local, token-level measure. A different failure mode exists: the model may be *confidently wrong* (high-confidence wrong answer), which is invisible to entropy.

An alternative signal: **consistency under repeated sampling**. If Haiku gives substantially different answers across K independent samples (temperature=0.7), that divergence signals model uncertainty — regardless of whether any individual sample is high-confidence. This is inspired by ensemble disagreement methods in uncertainty quantification (deep ensembles, Monte Carlo dropout).

## Mechanism

1. For each query, call Haiku K=3 times at temperature=0.7.
2. Compute pairwise BLEU or semantic similarity (sentence-transformer cosine similarity) between the K responses.
3. Consistency score = mean pairwise similarity. Low consistency → high divergence → escalate.
4. Threshold on consistency score: learned on a calibration set (binary search over threshold maximizing quality_at_budget_normalized on held-out split).
5. Escalate to Sonnet if consistency < θ_1; escalate to Opus if consistency < θ_2 (two-level cascade).

## Why divergence beats entropy

- Handles the "confidently wrong" failure mode (high per-sample entropy would miss this if each sample is individually confident but they disagree).
- More robust: K samples provide a richer signal than one forward pass.
- Orthogonal to HYP-001: can be combined (ensemble both signals) for an additive gain.

## Cost Analysis

- Overhead: K=3 Haiku calls per query vs. 1 Haiku call for entropy routing.
- Break-even: if even 20% of queries would have been sent to Sonnet/Opus unnecessarily under entropy routing, avoiding 20% of Sonnet/Opus calls more than pays for 2 extra Haiku calls.

## Experimental Setup

- Dataset: TruthfulQA (adversarial, high rate of confident-wrong failures), MMLU, GSM8K
- Baseline: HYP-001 entropy router (and text-feature RouteLLM) at matched cost
- Metric: quality_at_budget_normalized (same as HYP-001 for cross-hypothesis comparison)
- Seeds: [42, 123, 456]
- Expected: ≥4pp improvement over entropy baseline; especially large on TruthfulQA

## Open Questions

- Optimal K: K=3 balances overhead vs. signal quality, but K=5 may be better for highly adversarial queries.
- Should consistency be measured on full responses or just the first sentence/answer token?
