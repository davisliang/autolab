## Result: EXP-002 — Selective Speculation FAILS Pre-Registered Threshold

### Sanity Gate

Logistic classifier trained on 32 examples: loss 0.6931 → 0.1531, **77.91% drop** (threshold ≥50%). **PASS.**

### Multi-Seed Results (seeds: 42, 123, 456)

| Seed | Δ pp (proposed−baseline) | Proposed savings | Baseline savings | Accuracy Δ | Ambig zone |
|------|--------------------------|-----------------|-----------------|------------|------------|
| 42   | +6.01                    | 23.42 pp        | 17.41 pp        | −1.97 pp   | 23.3%      |
| 123  | +7.14                    | 23.82 pp        | 16.68 pp        | −1.97 pp   | 49.2%      |
| 456  | +4.63                    | 23.80 pp        | 19.17 pp        | −1.36 pp   | 30.9%      |
| **Mean** | **+5.93 ± 1.26**    | **23.68 pp**    | **17.75 pp**    | **−1.77 pp** | **34.4%** |

### Verdict

**FAIL** — pre-registered threshold was ≥8pp. Mean delta = **5.93pp**.

### Why it improved over EXP-001 but fell short

EXP-001 ("always speculate") lost −5.72pp vs baseline. EXP-002 (selective speculation) gains +5.93pp. The swing is +11.65pp — a large recovery — attributable to:

1. **Hard zone elimination**: ~50% of queries routed directly to Sonnet, avoiding the 1.2× Haiku+judge overhead that hurt EXP-001.
2. **Easy zone savings**: ~20% of queries routed directly to Haiku with no judge cost.
3. **Residual drag**: The ambiguous zone (~34% of queries) still incurs Haiku+judge overhead, and ~50% of those escalate anyway (FPR leakage + hard-for-Haiku queries in the band).

The structural fix worked; the magnitude was insufficient to clear 8pp at the strict quality constraint (−2pp tolerance). The baseline router is competitive because it implicitly "triages" via threshold without judge overhead.

### Implications for HYP-002

HYP-002 posited that output-quality signals (judge) provide enough information to overcome routing cost overhead by ≥8pp. The two experiments collectively show:
- Pure speculation (EXP-001): −5.72pp (costs dominate)
- Selective speculation (EXP-002): +5.93pp (costs reduced but margin too small)

The hypothesis is **rejected at the pre-registered threshold**. However, the positive direction in EXP-002 suggests a weaker finding: selective speculation is a **Pareto improvement** over both always-Haiku and always-Sonnet, just not as large as predicted. This is worth noting in the paper as a "preliminary" result with a revised claim.
