## Relevance to HYP-005

Triage is the most directly structurally parallel paper to HYP-005. It:
1. Uses exactly the same tier metaphor (light/standard/heavy ≈ Haiku/Sonnet/Opus)
2. Studies routing conditions analytically (when does Sonnet earn its place?)
3. Shows that middle tier is only cost-effective if (i) it can handle a meaningful slice of queries that the light tier fails on, and (ii) the signal can discriminate those queries

**Key gap**: Triage operates on SWE-bench (code tasks) and does not draw or compare Pareto curves for 3-tier vs 2-tier routing. It also does not report matched-cost accuracy improvements. HYP-005 tests this on MMLU with a Pareto frontier comparison.

Triage's analytical result is a useful theoretical prior for HYP-005: the middle tier earns its complexity when there is a non-trivial "Sonnet-only zone" (queries that Haiku fails but Sonnet handles) that the router can reliably identify.
