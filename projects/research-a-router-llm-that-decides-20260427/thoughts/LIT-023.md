## Relevance to HYP-005

This comprehensive 2025 survey directly maps the multi-LLM routing landscape. The key finding for HYP-005 is that virtually all deployed systems studied are **binary** (route to one of two models), with multi-tier routing explicitly flagged as an open challenge. The survey provides:

- **Taxonomy**: Difficulty-aware routing (Shnitzer, HybridLLM, RouteLLM) are all 2-model; the survey notes no systematic study comparing 2-tier vs 3-tier Pareto frontiers.
- **Avengers-Pro precedent**: Cluster-based routing over multiple models (not just binary) establishes a Pareto frontier surpassing strong individual models, offering indirect evidence that adding tiers can extend the achievable Pareto space.
- **Cascade results**: RouterBench shows cascading routers with multiple stages outperform binary cascades when the judge error rate is low (<0.1).

The survey does not provide a direct binary-vs-ternary comparison on MMLU, but it confirms that HYP-005 is testing an under-studied configuration.
