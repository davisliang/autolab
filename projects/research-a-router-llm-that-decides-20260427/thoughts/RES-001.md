## ExperimentResult: EXP-001

**Status: FAIL** — HYP-002 prediction not met.

### Primary metric
`cost_savings_pp_vs_feature_router` = **-5.72 ± 1.64 pp** (n=3 seeds)

Threshold was ≥+8pp. The speculative cascade is **worse** than input-feature routing by ~5.7pp.

### Per-seed breakdown

| Seed | Proposed savings (pp) | Baseline savings (pp) | Delta (pp) | Acc delta vs Sonnet (pp) |
|------|-----------------------|-----------------------|------------|--------------------------|
| 42   | 12.01                 | 19.29                 | -7.28      | -1.74                    |
| 123  | 12.32                 | 18.20                 | -5.88      | -1.82                    |
| 456  | 15.35                 | 19.35                 | -4.00      | -1.97                    |

### Root cause analysis

The structural overhead kills the approach:
- Speculative cascade pays **Haiku (1.0) + judge (0.2) = 1.2** on every query, even ones that must escalate
- Escalated queries cost **1.2 + 5.0 = 6.2** vs direct Sonnet at **5.0** — a 24% overhead
- To break even, the cascade must *not* escalate the ~24% of queries the input-feature router routes to Haiku
- In practice, the judge operates at TPR ~0.74-0.78, accepting only ~36-39% of queries as Haiku-worthy
- The input-feature router achieves ~22-24% Haiku rate while maintaining quality — it routes fewer but more-certain-easy queries
- Net effect: baseline saves ~19pp; cascade saves only ~13pp

### Accuracy constraint
Both strategies stay within QUALITY_TOLERANCE_PP = 2pp of Sonnet-only accuracy. Mean accuracy delta = -1.84pp.

### Sanity gate
Passed: cross-entropy loss 0.785 → 0.153 (80.5% drop on 32 examples; threshold ≥50%).

### Implication for HYP-002
The transplant from speculative decoding fails because the cost structure is fundamentally different: in token-level speculative decoding, the draft model generates for free (batched), so the verifier provides pure upside. At the model level, the cheap model costs real money on every query, and the escalation premium is large (5x cost ratio). The information advantage from seeing output quality is real but insufficient to overcome structural overhead in this parameter regime.
