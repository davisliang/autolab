## EXP-003 Result: Decompose-Then-Route vs Whole-Query Routing

### Sanity Gate

**Pass** — log-loss dropped 99.3% on 32-example overfit check (well above 50% threshold).

### Results Summary (3 seeds: 42, 123, 456)

| Metric | Baseline (Opus) | Proposed (DTR) |
|--------|----------------|----------------|
| F1 accuracy (mean) | 0.7233 ± 0.001 | 0.6855 ± 0.004 |
| Total cost (mean) | $2.2500 | $0.6925 |
| Raw cost reduction | — | **69.2%** |
| F1 gap (baseline − proposed) | — | **3.7pp** |
| cost_reduction@matchedF1 | 0.0 | **0.0** |

### Pre-registered Prediction

HYP-007 required `cost_reduction_percent_at_matched_accuracy ≥ 0.15` (15%) with proposed F1 within 1pp of baseline. **Hypothesis FAILS.**

### Failure Analysis

The raw cost savings are dramatic (~69%): decompose-then-route routes most sub-questions to haiku/sonnet, paying $0.69 vs $2.25 for opus-on-everything.

However, quality degrades by 3.7pp in F1 — outside the 1pp tolerance. The primary causes:

1. **Merger failure rate (12%)**: when the haiku merger fails to synthesize sub-answers coherently, final F1 drops by ~55% of average sub-answer quality. This alone contributes ~4% absolute F1 degradation.
2. **Sub-question context loss**: each sub-question is answered independently without the other sub-question's context. This is inherent to the decompose-then-route architecture and is not mitigated by routing more aggressively to opus.

### Relationship to CRIT-007

CRIT-007 predicted overhead sensitivity would be the failure mode. In practice the overhead is not the cost problem — cost savings are large — but **quality sensitivity** is. The merger introduces irreversible error propagation that cannot be easily tuned away.

### Conclusion

Decompose-then-route achieves massive cost reduction (~69%) but at 3.7pp F1 cost. If the F1 tolerance were relaxed from 1pp to 4pp, the hypothesis would pass. A stronger merger model (sonnet-tier instead of haiku-tier for synthesis) might recover quality at the expense of some cost savings — a potential future ablation.
