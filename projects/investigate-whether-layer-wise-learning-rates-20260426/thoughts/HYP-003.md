## Motivation

Shallow layers extract low-level features that converge quickly and should not be over-written by later fine-tuning signals. Deeper (task-specific) layers need finer updates because they live close to the loss. A geometric decay mirrors empirical fine-tuning practice where the last layer is given the highest LR and earlier layers are frozen or given near-zero LR — here we invert that bias for random init because early features need large initial displacement.

## Mechanism

LR_l = base_lr * gamma^l where l ∈ {0, 1, 2} for a 3-layer MLP. Grid-search gamma ∈ {0.3, 0.5, 0.7} and report best.

## Pre-registration

Best-gamma val_accuracy after 20 epochs ≥ uniform SGD val_accuracy + 0.3 pp. Baseline: SGD lr=0.01.
