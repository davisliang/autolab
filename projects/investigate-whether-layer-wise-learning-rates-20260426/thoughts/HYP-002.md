## Motivation

Layers that produce large gradients are already updating weights aggressively; giving them a smaller LR acts as automatic stabilisation while under-stimulated layers (small gradients) get a proportionally larger nudge. This is a first-principles application of the "equalise effective step size" intuition.

## Mechanism

At each step, compute per-layer gradient L2-norm g_l. Set LR_l = base_lr / (g_l + eps). Clip to [base_lr/10, base_lr*10] to avoid instability.

## Pre-registration

Final val_accuracy after 20 epochs ≥ uniform-LR baseline + 0.5 percentage points. Baseline: SGD, lr=0.01, no momentum.
